What direct hire means here

We find the people who never answer a job posting

The best maintenance and automation professionals are already employed and not looking. They don’t apply — they get recruited. Our entire model is built to reach them, vet them technically, and bring them to your team as permanent employees.

  • Permanent, full-time placements — your employee from day one.
  • Passive candidate sourcing through a deep manufacturing network.
  • Technical screening by recruiters who’ve done the work.
  • 6-month placement guarantee on every search.
Why direct hire beats generic staffing

We target, we don’t post

Job boards reach the 20% who are actively looking. We go after the 80% who aren’t — the technicians and engineers already doing the job well somewhere else.

We screen technically

We ask the questions you would ask: PLC platforms, troubleshooting approach, controls architecture, reliability methods. Resumes don’t tell you who can actually do it.

We guarantee the fit

Every placement is backed by a 6-month guarantee. We’re not filling a seat — we’re building your team, and we stand behind it.

Our Process

A proven, technical search process

Companies want to know how the work gets done. Here is exactly how we run every search — from the plant floor to follow-up.

Discovery

We walk your floor (in person or virtually), learn your equipment, shift structure, and what “good” actually looks like for the role.

Technical Recruiting

We tap our manufacturing network and target the passive technicians and engineers who never apply to job boards.

Candidate Screening

Every candidate is vetted for stability, motivation, shift fit, and the practical skills the role demands.

Technical Interview

We interview on PLCs, controls, troubleshooting and reliability — because we’ve done the work ourselves.

Client Interview

We present a short list of qualified, interested candidates and coordinate the entire interview loop for you.

Offer

We manage the offer, counter-offer and resignation conversation so your hire actually shows up on day one.

Follow-Up

We check in through onboarding and the full guarantee period to make sure the placement sticks.
