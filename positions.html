How we screen

We interview on the work, not the keywords

Anyone can list “PLC” on a resume. We talk through real scenarios — a line down, an intermittent fault, a reliability problem — and listen for how a candidate actually thinks and troubleshoots.

  • Platform fluency: Allen-Bradley, Siemens, and the controls stack you run.
  • Hands-on troubleshooting and electrical diagnostics.
  • Reliability methods: CMMS, TPM, RCM, predictive maintenance.
  • Shift fit, stability and motivation — so the placement sticks.
