Our Process

A proven, technical search process

Companies want to know how the work gets done. Here is exactly how we run every search — from the plant floor to follow-up.

Discovery

We walk your floor (in person or virtually), learn your equipment, shift structure, and what “good” actually looks like for the role.

Technical Recruiting

We tap our manufacturing network and target the passive technicians and engineers who never apply to job boards.

Candidate Screening

Every candidate is vetted for stability, motivation, shift fit, and the practical skills the role demands.

Technical Interview

We interview on PLCs, controls, troubleshooting and reliability — because we’ve done the work ourselves.

Client Interview

We present a short list of qualified, interested candidates and coordinate the entire interview loop for you.

Offer

We manage the offer, counter-offer and resignation conversation so your hire actually shows up on day one.

Follow-Up

We check in through onboarding and the full guarantee period to make sure the placement sticks.
